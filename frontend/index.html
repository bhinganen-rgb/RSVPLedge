How the pledge works

Three moves. On-chain. No middleman holds the money — the contract does.

01

Host sets the deposit

Pick a cap, a cutoff, an event date, and how the forfeited pool splits between you and the guests who showed.

02

Guests pledge in

RSVP by posting the deposit. Cancel before cutoff for a full refund. After cutoff, the guest list is locked.

03

Show up, get more back

Host marks attendance. Attendees claim their deposit and split the flakes' forfeits as a show-up bonus.
